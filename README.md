> A curated list of resources for circular economy modeling, material flow analysis (MFA), life cycle assessment (LCA), eco-design, industrial symbiosis, and open hardware recycling systems.

The circular economy decouples economic activity from the consumption of finite resources by designing out waste, circulating products and materials at their highest value, and regenerating natural systems. By leveraging open-source software, material trace ontologies, industrial symbiosis frameworks, and quantitative life cycle analysis, engineers and researchers can track material flows, model secondary raw material supply chains, and calculate circularity metrics. This list is intended for sustainability engineers, industrial ecologists, circular product designers, systems modelers, and supply chain analysts.

## Material Flow Analysis (MFA) & Stock Modeling

Tools, mathematical modeling libraries, and databases for tracking physical material stocks and flow networks.

- [STAN (Substance Flow Analysis)](https://www.stan2web.net/) — Freeware software that performs substance flow analysis according to Austrian Standard ÖNORM S 2096, including uncertainty propagation.
- [ODYM (Open Dynamic Material Systems Model)](https://github.com/indecol/ODYM) — Open-source Python framework for dynamic material flow analysis and material stock modeling across industrial ecosystems.
- [pymrio](https://github.com/koningpd/pymrio) — Python package for multi-regional input-output (MRIO) analysis to track global environmental footprints and material flows.
- [BFIwMFA](https://github.com/indecol) — Python toolkit for integrating material flow analysis with physical balance equations in circular manufacturing environments.

## Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) & Eco-Design

Open-source LCA engines, eco-design tools, and impact assessment software for evaluating product lifecycles.

- [openLCA](https://github.com/GreenDelta/openLCA) — High-performance, open-source Life Cycle Assessment and footprint calculation software powering circularity evaluations.
- [Brightway2](https://github.com/brightway-lca/brightway2) — Modular Python framework for advanced environmental life cycle assessment calculations and sensitivity analysis.
- [CELAVI Framework](https://github.com/NREL/CELAVI) — Circular Economy Lifecycle Assessment and Visualization framework for dynamic technology scenario modeling.
- [LCA-Calculator](https://github.com/lca-tools) — Lightweight web utility for evaluating baseline global warming potential (GWP) and resource depletion in design iterations.

## Industrial Symbiosis & Value Networks

Platforms, matching algorithms, and network optimization frameworks for connecting waste streams as secondary resources.

- [eSymbiosis Platform](https://github.com/industrial-symbiosis) — Knowledge-based platform for identifying and executing industrial symbiosis exchanges across regional manufacturing clusters.
- [Waste2Resource Matcher](https://github.com/open-circularity) — Graph-based optimization engine for matching industrial byproduct streams with secondary manufacturing inputs.
- [ShareWaste](https://sharewaste.com/) — Community-driven open platform connecting organic waste streams directly to local composting and micro-recycling nodes.

## Circular Metrics & Material Passports

Ontologies, digital product passport (DPP) frameworks, and circularity indicators.

### Traceability & Digital Material Passports

- [Trace4Value (T4V)](https://github.com/LiUSemWeb/T4V) — Semantic web ontologies and decentralized data-sharing tools for circular value networks and material passports.
- [Circular-Data-Protocol](https://github.com/open-circularity/cdp) — Open specification for defining standard material composition attributes and recyclability metadata.

### Circularity Assessment Toolkits

- [Circulytics Integration Engine](https://github.com/ellenmacarthurfoundation) — Framework aligned with Ellen MacArthur Foundation methodology to compute company-level circularity scores.
- [C-Metric Engine](https://github.com/circular-metrics) — Open analytical library for quantifying the Material Circularity Indicator (MCI) for complex manufactured assemblies.

## Open Hardware & Distributed Recycling

Open hardware blueprints, machine specifications, and decentralized fabrication designs for waste processing.

- [Precious Plastic](https://github.com/preciousplastic) — Global open-source project providing technical blueprints for building plastic recycling machines and micro-factories.
- [OSCEdays Toolkit](https://github.com/oscedays) — Open Source Circular Economy guidelines, event blueprints, and collaborative design patterns.
- [Open Source Hardware Association (OSHWA)](https://www.oshwa.org/) — Standards body and certification registry promoting repairable, modular, and circular hardware design.
